Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Cash on Delivery work for equipment orders?
Cash on Delivery is available nationwide across Pakistan. You place your order online, our support team confirms delivery details via call or WhatsApp, the hardware is dispatched with our logistics partners (Trax / Leopard), and you pay cash to the rider upon delivery.
What are your delivery timelines across Pakistan?
Orders within Karachi are delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Shipments to Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, and Peshawar typically arrive within 2 to 4 business days.
Are products covered by official warranty?
Yes. Motorized treadmills come with an official 2-year motor and 1-year electronics warranty. Structural equipment including power cages, commercial squat racks, and Olympic bars include lifetime structural weld coverage.
Can I visit your Karachi showroom to test equipment before buying?
Absolutely! Our Saddar, Karachi demo showroom features live workout demo units for commercial treadmills, air bikes, adjustable dumbbells, and power racks. Visit us Monday through Saturday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
Do you offer commercial gym setup packages and wholesale discounts?
Yes! We supply commercial gymnasiums, sports complexes, and corporate fitness centers with custom packages, floor layout consulting, and tiered wholesale pricing. Select "Gym Setup & Bulk" on the form above or reach out on WhatsApp (+92 300 0000000).
