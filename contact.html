Frequently Asked Questions

How does Cash on Delivery work for equipment orders?
Cash on Delivery is available across Pakistan. You place your order online, we confirm and dispatch it with our courier partners, and you pay cash to the delivery rider upon package arrival.
What are your delivery timelines?
Orders within Lahore are typically delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Orders to Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and other major cities arrive within 2 to 4 business days.
Are products covered by warranty?
Yes. Motorized treadmills include an official 2-year motor warranty. Structural equipment such as power cages and benches feature frame durability coverage.
